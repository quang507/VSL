```text
XEM NGƯỜI THẬT KÝ
• 100 ký hiệu thông dụng, mỗi từ có video người thật ký mẫu.

TỪ ĐIỂN 3.315 TỪ

• Thẻ ghi nhớ, đố vui, 59 câu giao tiếp thường ngày.
```

```text
VSLV helps Deaf and hard-of-hearing people, and their families, learn Vietnamese Sign Language (VSL) on a phone.

WATCH REAL SIGNERS
• 100 everyday signs, each with a video of a real signer.
• Slow playback at 0.5x and looping, so hand shape and movement are easy to follow.
• Nine stages: greetings, people, food, time, feelings, body, travel, actions and health.

SIGN TO THE CAMERA
• Stand about one metre away, copy the sign, and the app tells you whether it recognised it.
• Live hints while you sign: move your hands into the frame, step back, come closer.
• Recognition is still being improved and can be wrong, especially in low light. The app always shows which sign it detected.

BUILT FOR DEAF USERS
• Feedback uses haptics and a coloured screen edge. Sound is optional.
• Large text, large buttons and high contrast.
• Translate mode (experimental): sign continuously and the app shows text and reads it aloud for hearing people.

A 3,315-WORD DICTIONARY
• Browse the Vietnamese Ministry of Education and Training sign dictionary with videos, meanings and parts of speech.
• Choose the Northern, Central or Southern variant of a sign.
• Search without typing accents. Save words for later.

REMEMBER MORE
• Spaced repetition brings each sign back just before you would forget it.
• Flashcards, quizzes and 59 everyday phrases.
• Daily goal, streaks and badges.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account.
• The camera is processed on your device. Images and video are never stored or uploaded.
• No ads and no tracking.
• Lessons and scoring work offline. Only the extended dictionary needs a connection to stream videos.

Content sources: ViSignLanguage-Video (PTIT AI Challenge, CC-BY-4.0), the Look & Tell project (Apache-2.0), and the Ministry of Education and Training sign language dictionary.

Feedback: quanggooner1996@gmail.com
```

**Apple · App Privacy**

| Câu hỏi | Trả lời |
|---|---|
| App có thu thập dữ liệu không? | Không (Data Not Collected) |

Trả lời này chỉ đúng khi app không gắn công cụ đo nào. Nếu sau này bật Clarity hoặc PostHog thì phải khai lại, và sửa `ios/App/App/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`.
